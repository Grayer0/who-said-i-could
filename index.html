谁允许我买的？Who Said I Could?

把一次冲动消费变成一份需要陈述理由、等待回复的审批申请。一条申请可同时发送给多位好友，由多数意见决定结果。A WeChat Mini Program that turns an impulse purchase into an approval request — you state your case, your friends vote, and the majority decides. (The app itself is in Chinese; screens below are the real thing.)

两套主题共用同一组语义色位——主色、批准、驳回、再想想，区别只在冷暖和饱和度，所以切换之后每个状态还是原来的意思。Both themes share one semantic palette — accent, approve, reject, hold. Only warmth and saturation differ, so a colour never changes what it means.

怎么用How it works

扫码打开，或者自己部署一份——它跑在微信云开发上，不需要服务器。Scan to open, or run your own copy — it sits on WeChat Cloud, so there is no server to keep alive.

  1. 把小程序分享给家人或朋友，他们打开就能表态，不用注册。Share it with a friend. They open the card and weigh in — no signup.
  2. 写申请时理由是必填的。很多东西在写理由的时候就自己消失了。Writing the request forces you to state a reason. A lot of things quietly disappear at that step.
  3. 结果按少数服从多数算，票数变了结果跟着变，平票计为「再想想」。The outcome follows the majority, updating live as votes come in. A tie counts as “think it over”.
  4. 没有广告，没有第三方统计，所有申请记录只保存在你自己的账号下。No ads, no third-party analytics. Every record stays in your own account.

冲动是一阵子的，账单是一整年的。把「想买」变成一次要写理由、要等回复的申请，很多东西在写理由的时候就自己消失了。An impulse lasts a moment; the bill lasts all year. Turn “I want this” into a request that needs a reason and an answer, and a surprising number of things vanish while you type.
